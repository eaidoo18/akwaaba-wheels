Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our services

What do I need to rent a car?

A valid driving licence (Ghana or international), a Ghana Card or passport, and a deposit by MoMo or card.

How old do I have to be?

Drivers must be 23 or older and have held a licence for at least two years.

Can I pay with Mobile Money?

Yes. We accept MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money, cards and cash.

Can I drive outside Accra?

Yes, anywhere in Ghana. Tell us your route so we can give you the right car and advice.
